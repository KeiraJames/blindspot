Privacy policy

Draft for review. Replace the bracketed parts before publishing.

Camera and LiDAR

The camera and LiDAR scanner are processed on your device. While you navigate, no images or depth data leave your phone.

Room scans

When you save a room scan to a shared map, the room layout, a 3D model, an AR world map and a simplified mesh are uploaded to our database (Google Firebase) so people you share the map with can use it. You can delete a map at any time.

Voice

Spoken directions are generated by ElevenLabs. The text of each direction (for example "Turn left in 5 meters") is sent to ElevenLabs through our server. No audio from your microphone is sent to ElevenLabs. You can turn off the natural voice in Settings to use the built-in iOS voice only.

Speech recognition

When you say a room name or destination, Apple's speech recognition processes it, on your device whenever the device supports it.
