Terms of use

Draft for review by a qualified person before publishing.

PathSense is a travel aid, not a mobility device. It complements a long cane or guide dog and does not replace them, orientation and mobility training, or your own judgment.

Maps are made by people and can be wrong or out of date. Obstacle detection cannot find every hazard, and PathSense never confirms that a path is clear.

Only scan places you have permission to scan. Do not scan people.
